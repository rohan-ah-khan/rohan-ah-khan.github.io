About

{% capture about_md %} I’m a bioinformatician at the [Centre for Computational Medicine (CCM)](https://ccm.sickkids.ca/team/) at the Hospital for Sick Children. CCM is a core facility within SickKids, and my work there spans developing pipelines for processing and analyzing genomic data, machine learning applications with imaging, and bioinformatics consulting for research teams at the hospital and beyond. Previously, I worked at Princess Margaret Cancer Centre building pipelines to analyze mutational burden in complex and large follicular lymphoma cohorts (Kridel Lab), and prior to that at the University of Calgary in the Geomicrobiology group, where I worked on microbial genomic data and their roles in hydrocarbon systems, tracking microbial communities across environments and building ML models to predict hydrocarbon presence in offshore exploration using microbial community data. I completed my B.Sc. at the University of Calgary in Cellular, Molecular and Microbial Biology with a strong focus on computer science, followed by an M.H.Sc. at the University of Toronto in Medical Genomics, where I worked on variant analysis, science communication, and the applications of genomics in medicine. Outside of work: Formula 1, reading, and exploring financial markets. {% endcapture %} {{ about_md | markdownify }}
