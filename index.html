About

{% capture about_md %} I am a bioinformatics and medical genomics researcher with experience spanning whole-genome sequencing and microbiome datasets. My work combines computational pipelines, statistical genomics, and machine learning to analyze complex biological data and uncover clinically and biologically relevant insights. At the Princess Margaret Cancer Centre, I focus on cancer genomics by analyzing targeted sequencing data from patient cohorts to characterize mutational profiles, subclonal architecture, mutation burden, copy number alterations, and ploidy. I developed and optimized multi-caller consensus pipelines covering alignment, quality control, variant calling, and structural variant detection on HPC systems (SLURM/Linux), with scripting in Perl and Python to streamline workflow automation. Previously at the University of Calgary, I developed machine learning models in R to integrate 16S rRNA sequencing data with ecological metadata, achieving predictive accuracy across global validation sites. My research also involved statistical analyses (NMDS, regression modeling) to connect microbial community structure with environmental features, and HMM-based functional annotation to identify pathways in metagenome datasets.
